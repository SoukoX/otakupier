About OtakuPier

OtakuPier is a catalog and community board for anime and manga. Where most sites are just a grid of posters and a search bar, this one is built around a simple idea: the people watching should shape what everyone finds.

Every title here links out to a way to watch it. Members vote on community rankings, leave reviews, keep watchlists, and talk in the live chat. And the more you take part, the more the site reflects you — earned Reward Points let you customize your name color, title, avatar glow, and even a VIP badge.
