OtakuPier leaderboards

Compete with the community across three leaderboards. Review anime, chat, and take part to climb the ranks — the more you contribute, the higher you climb.

  • Top reviewers — the members who have written the most anime reviews.
  • Top contributors — the fans with the most experience points from daily logins, reviews, votes, and chat.
  • Top earners — the members who have earned the most Reward Points, including through the invite system.

Each board deep-links to its own tab, so you can share a direct link to any ranking category.
